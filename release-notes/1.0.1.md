## Fixes

- If your menu bar is full, macOS can hide the DictateMute icon behind the camera notch. Now you can always reach DictateMute: open it again from Spotlight, Finder, or Launchpad, and its window appears with all its settings.
- DictateMute shows its window the first time you open it, so you know it is running.
- Launch at Login is now on by default, since DictateMute only helps while it is running. You can turn it off in the window.
